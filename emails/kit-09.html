Hello to my lovely college mates and Keepintouch readers!

I’m writing this in my front yard at sunset watching my gay cats chase each other around and fight(maybe some internalized homophobia I think)

It has been a busy summer for me since graduation but I’m also more bored than ever.

Right after graduation, I went off to Europe with my friend Brian and backpacked to seven different countries for 3 and a half weeks. It was quite an adventure. Family and friends always ask me what city was my favorite, the most beautiful or the most fun, but the real test is which city has the best public transportation and gay bars. And boy was I happy to investigate.

First, I’ll start with the more *boring* category but still exciting; public transportation. Ok honestly they were all very amazing, the London Underground and double decker buses were probably my favorite because it’s so iconic and huge. That design system is really something(thanks David(he didn’t do anything he’s just British)). It feels like a basic answer but I will speak my truth.

Secondly and more importantly, the gay bars. We went to a few joints in each city except for Budapest and Porto. I would like to say it’s a contest between the British, the French and even the Czech. But Madrid clears by miles. SO many gay bars, lesbian bars, clubs, and even a gay metro stop(see me pictured above in front of the metro entrance). Definitely hit up Chueca if you guys ever find yourselves in Madrid.

Once I got back I started my remote internship with the ACLU! So far it’s been pretty chill. I’ve been working on various merch projects and instagram graphics. It is weird being fully remote though. I don’t feel super connected to everyone, which I don’t love. We also design basically everything in Figma, which if you know me, you know I love Figma but designing everything in it is a little weird. It slows me down a little but I think overall I’m learning so much about Figma which is great, and I will be speedy as usual again soon.

I have been listening to so many podcasts while I work because I cannot sit in silence alone in my room. I listened to a Paula Scher interview today actually and she is such a baddie and icon. She’s really just like all of us <3. If you guys have any podcast recs pls send them my way. Oh my gosh I’m also so locked in on the World Cup. Before this I think I had maybe watched two full games of soccer *football* in my life and now I have been watching almost all the games since the group stage ended. I don’t know what’s happening to me. I actually watch while I’m working which is so bad but it’s an addiction. Rest in peace USA, the boys will be back and Spain will smoke Belgium next game, so all will be right.

Outside of work I’m trying to soak up my Flawda summer and go to the beach, lake or springs as much as possible. I’m scared for my future of work but for now it’s summer, and I’ll figure out everything else later. I hope you are all doing well and being kind to yourselves out there in the big scary world. Slay the day away. Byeeee.

What's your best freelancing advice or tips on developing a price sheet now that we don't always have to charge student prices?

  • Looking up typical rates are for the service while keeping in mind the client's size and industry (like charging less for local / nonprofit work)
  • Looking back at Eury’s Miro and winging it
  • Emailing Eury
  • Never under-valuing your services, never being intimidated by the client, and setting a flat fee for different kinds of projects. If your hourly rate is high, the client might say yes but ask for a tighter deadline to curb costs.
