Hello party people, once again!

Alright, so from where I left off I had left GNV Florida. Since I have no clue when I’ll be back in Miami, I did a 2 day trip to check on all the places I grew up and hang out with my friends back there!

I ate my weight in rice and beans, got Pollo Tropical for the road, and tried to see Spiderman Brand New day- but it was totally sold out, so no dice. I went to the Falls, which has gotten really bougie (for my uninitiated the Falls is this huge outdoor shopping mall that used to be THE place for middle/highschoolers in South Miami), and said bye to my Miami friends. Thankfully after 4 years of college in different cities, I think I can be friends with them no matter where we are.

PS: For my Central to South Florida people— did y’all know that Pollo Tropical is ONLY EXCLUSIVE TO FLORIDA? Guys I was devastated to find this out, where will I get my cheap mid Caribbean food?

And then… It was time.

So I arrived in Chicago, hauled my ass with two suitcases across the city from Midway airport to Chinatown and back to get my keys from the leasing office, and FINALLY, finally arrived at my apartment. Only to realize one teensy tiny issue. In my lease it’s stipulated that any moving MUST be done through the back of the building to keep from blocking the front. I only toured from the front stairs/entrance of the building obviously, because why would I need to see where the trash bins are?

So the back of the building:

Yeah.

Anyways, after moving multiple 50 lb suitcases, trash bags of clothing, toiletries, art supplies, and furniture non-stop for 12 hrs (because my moving service was like: mama once you start unpacking the cube, you gotta be done by the end of the day) I can confirm, fire escape stairs are sturdier (but more slippery) than they first appear!

Since Moving to Chicago Highlights:

The first thing you have to understand is that the sun rises at 5:30 AM here. I am gooped, I am gagged. As a night owl this is tragic to me personally- what do you mean at 7 am the sun is FULLY UP???

On an unrelated note, I acquired my primary my needs for Big City living including:

  1. Fans (because I have no central or Window AC. You’d think I’d have died of heat stroke by now, but these people are so lucky— it never goes above 88! BUT I’ll have to update my opinion on the weather once the infamous winter hits).
  2. Internet (shoutout to the Xfinity worker who had to LEAN OUT of my 3rd story window to grab the cable to wire my place).

Also, for one moment, I must put the dwellers of the Great City of Chicago on blast. These people have NO clue how good they have it, I fear. There are bus stops literally every block. I can reach downtown in an hour and a half TOPS. I have been on a pilgrimage to many a gay bar, and to go from the Southwest (where I live) to the North East (where the other gays live) takes an hour and half max for 20 miles. Now the car-based among you might scoff at these numbers. But guys. If you recall, I used to work at Publix. My Publix was FIVE miles away from me, and it would take me an hour MINIMUM to get to work every weekend.

As my friend Roe (from South Miami) says, “a TRUE Walker isn't someone who lives in a walkable area with nice transit, it’s actually someone who lives in a hostile area where you have to walk stretches on the highway to get to your bus stop”. None of these people are True Walkers. I met someone here who said if the place was over 30 MINUTES of public transit time from his house he wasn’t going (mind you, in Miami my primary bus stop was a 30 minute walk away)…I rest my case.

In sillier news! I’ve been on a Park crusade, because nature is cool and fun! Also they have beaches here! (Live picture of me crashing out while I get lost on the way to the beach).


I will end this two parter diatribe with a measure of understanding. I have lived here for approaching 1 month, and totally understand why it’s called the Windy City. The updraft from the buildings closer to Lake Michigan is actually horrendous, but fingers crossed I acclimate by the time winter rolls around!

See y’all next time for further Chicago Adventures!
