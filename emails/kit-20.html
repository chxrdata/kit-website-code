Hi friends!

So much has happened since my last news letter, I must update you all again. First, I’ll say that I miss you guys and our wonderful professors. Reading everyone’s newsletters makes me warm and fuzzy. I’m lucky to know you all and miss you all.

Today, I am writing to y’all from Meridian or Malcolm X park in Washington DC, which is where I live now. I moved up here two and a half weeks ago for an internship! It was a very quick decision but honestly I wanted a job and as it turns out I got a job here. I’m working for a political and nonprofit fundraising company that basically deals in donor data to help democrats raise money to win elections. It’s pretty cool so far and I’m learning a lot about political campaigns, especially in fundraising. It’s completely in person which I like SO MUCH more than my remote job.

Outside of work, I am trying to get into a routine and try new things plus explore. It’s been cold this week, which is crazy for me to experience such weather in September(so early). I’ve also been reminded how no one in Florida owns coats… including myself. Like it’s 55 degrees and raining and people are in nice coats here. But in Florida I know people would just wear a sweatshirt and call it a day.

I’ve also started biking to work sometimes. But there's something crucial worth noting, hills. Hills make biking horrible. I am happy to save the money on the metro and be outside on the bike but this hill right before my apartment makes it literally impossible. Today I actually just walked my bike up the hill because it was easier.

Before I got here I took a road trip from Orlando to Maine with my tall and dear friend Ruby. She has a job up there so we made it a little adventure. It was really awesome, I would recommend it if you won’t see your best friend for a long time. We made it up to Maine in three and a half days. We stopped in Raleigh, Philadelphia and Boston. I got to see Macy in Boston which was awesome. We stayed four nights around Portland, which was a very chill small town. Side note if anyone ever comes through DC you must text me please.

I’ve been really prioritizing my media consumption because I don’t have many friends yet and I actually have time now. I’m reading Fourth Wing currently and I can’t lie, I am hooked. I finished the new season of Adults and Chad Powers. I just started Hacks and it’s great so far. Send me any current show or book obsessions, I would love to add some to the list.

I find it really funny that after my last news letter, I find myself in a city with a really great and beautiful metro system AND with a plethora of gay bars to explore. I can’t wait to investigate as the leaves change and share my findings with you all.

Sorry this was long and had no structure but I miss you guys. Please text with ways to improve my newsletter structure, discuss media or just say hey.
