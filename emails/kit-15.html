Hello party people!!!

You may recall last week I said, “Fun Fact! Fire escape stairs are sturdier than you think!”

Now what did I mean by that? Strap in, this is gonna be a long one, but first, we must rewind!

After graduation, I planned to stay in Gainesville for 3 months for the rest of my lease and to do my virtual internship (with a Mortgage company but a job is a job I fear— guys please help me manifest a design job I want in the next year).

After that the plan was simple— move to Chicago if I didn’t have a job after the internship, or move to NYC if I did get one. In the meantime, I hung out with a bunch of my BFA friends, since many of them are remaining in Gainesville for the time being.

I actually explored the areas around my house, which I did not do the whole year I lived at my house because famously, I was zonked out in the studio! (Turns out it’s actually super pretty).

Also, can we applaud that I became actual real life friends with Eury Kim, LETS GOOO! (Pictured: Us cooking mama-ing at each others houses)

Unfortunately, our amazing cooking sessions were cut short because she had to go to Alabama- but we still text :D

So that was most of my summer until July, in which I started to Lock In. I wasn’t able to get a junior position or another Internship, so that left Chicago! (The 20 something’s YEARN for good public transit).

Now here’s where it gets serious- it’s July 4th. And I have been calling, texting, emailing, etc to get an apartment in Chicago and ONE thing becomes clear through the irritated slum lords, thinly veiled warnings from the front desk clerk people (true story a lady was lowkey warning me not to stay at the apartment she was SCHEDULING the showing for) and my call log with my dad (my therapist).

I NEEDED to go in person to tour the places that I might be staying at. One problem— that’s going to cost $700 directly out of my moving fund. But my therapist put it this way, “Would you rather be $700 poorer, or literally homeless??”. So after that the plane tickets were booked, lol.

On July 11th at 12 am, I set off on a greyhound to the Orlando airport for my Crazy girlTM 48 hour Chicago trip. From arriving in Chicago at 8 am I:

  1. Toured 5 apartments in 8 hrs (which trust, sounds like a lot of time, but I was running across the South side).
  2. Gained a core memory: My hostel for the night is overbooked due to a scheduling issue. I go outside to update my therapist and he’s like “Omg girl are you homeless in Chicago AGAIN?!”. I am chilling on the sidewalk talking to him and a random lady pulls up in her car directly in front of me eating a green apple in one hand, the other hand on the wheel, and a massive dog hanging over her shoulder. The conversation goes as follows: “LAH! You’re Sarah?” “Yes?” “I’m here to take you to the other hostel, this one has no bed right?” “Yeah…let me get my stuff”. So I got in her car and went to the other hostel! (And this is that massive dog: her name is Yaya!)
  3. Turned 21!
  4. Realized how shit the Orlando bus system is SPECIFICALLY leaving the airport (truly that city was built by Walt in a lab because why is the only competent bus line to Disney Springs? Be so fr)

And then I got back home to GNV safe and sound on 12 am July 13th. A couple days later I actually celebrated turning 21! (Pictured: Straining the wine my therapist bought for me in 2005 because I somehow dropped the cork inside it).

Then, because we stay up- I GOT MY APARTMENT, LETS GO! (Don’t ask me how much money it took to actually do it please).

From then it was just a countdown of stuff to do: hang out with all my Art baddies one last time, pack a Relocube to send my stuff across the country, and because I was still working, complete a final group presentation to the C-suite at my job over Microsoft teams (I hear pinging in my nightmares guys, corporate is scary). Then, I cleaned out my room for the last time and said goodbye to GNV, Florida!

Now! I drafted this whole thing on a Google doc and am solidly over the word limit- so I’m breaking this into two parts!
