Hi guys! I hope everyone's summer is going well. A lot has changed over the course of mine. I considered many ways to talk about it, but I thought it might be more interesting to instead tell you about what has managed to stay the same. A good old listicle, if you will. I'm getting nostalgic for teen magazines.

Without further ado, here are five things that have stayed the same despite my now living in New York:

  1. I still buy the same groceries at more or less the same stores. I mean, technically I go to Lidl instead of Aldi, but three of those letters are the same. Lidl has worse cold brew but also sells pastries for 99 cents, so l'd say it's a fair trade. I'd go to Trader Joes for cold brew but there's actually not one in my neighborhood. Which is good from a gentrification standpoint!



  2. My hair is still blue and I still try to be very careful not to stain anything (especially because I'm staying in a sublet) but still fail sometimes. I did actually say I was going to wait until the end of summer to re-dye my hair but I was absolutely kidding myself. If you are ever dyeing your hair I do not recommend Splat; it is basically fabric dye and it will ruin your shirts. Pictured below is how I washed my hair for a week.



  3. I am still on my computer most of the time. I don't spend my entire 9-6 on it (we do some physical mockups to judge scale, and this week I was mounting stuff to a 6 foot wooden structure), but it still lures me on my off time. I blame Slay the Spire 2 a little bit but it is time I am honest with myself about my love for the puter. I like the terrible everything machine. I guess there are worse things.



  4. Regardless, I still touch grass! New York City is not as much of a concrete jungle as it's made out to be. There's a park by my apartment, there's a park by my work, and I recently found a community garden. A few weeks ago, I took a train only an hour north to Cold Spring, which had actual mountains!



  5. Finally, I am happy to be able to say that I still talk to all of you :) it has been a joy to compile everyone's newsletters and talk to you all outside the newsletter. Shoutout to Mads who I saw on Monday during their New York visit!



As the summer nears its end, I wanted to thank everyone who has written so far. I know there will be less to talk about with everyone home from their vacations, and maybe we won't publish every week. Regardless, I want to make this newsletter a place for both the spectacular and mundane. It's just as lovely to hear about you Europe trip as it is the new restaurant you found in your neighborhood! Most of life is largely unremarkable, but by no means is the unremarkable unimportant.

Thank you for keepingintouch!

Del
