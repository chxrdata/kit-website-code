Hello dear friends! I have absolutely loved getting a glimpse into y’all’s lives through this newsletter!

Here’s whats happening with me!

After a few weeks of relaxation (and continuous job searching) at home in Tallahassee after graduation, I received word that I had been offered an internship up in Virginia in the DC area with a defense contracting company. It was a little scary to go so far from home, but I decided to take it!

My mom and I drove up here and moved me into the room I am renting from a Peruvian lady who is an artist and a Spanish teacher, which has been nice to have someone to keep my spanish skills sharp. There are some quirks to the living situation, but its been great, and I love the little town I live in (Vienna).

The internship has been… well… fine. It's not the most interesting job, and it can be a lot of boring corporate stuff, but there have been some fun projects here and there. The 8-hr work days M-F have been really tough, but I'm getting used to it. Thankfully it pays pretty well for an internship, which is great. The marketing team consists of just me and my supervisor, so any creative/design work comes through us. So it’s small, but I'm still learning new things and the larger Business Development group that we are a part of is a very fun group. The internship contract ends in late August, so we'll see if I get offered some sort of extension.

Life in Virginia... It's still just about as humid as FL, but the summer heat is not quite so horrible. It will be interesting to see how I manage snow/ice if I end up staying here past the summer. I LOVE the thought of getting to experience actual seasons.

I've been exploring lots of local trails, hikes, coffee shops, and the occasional outing to DC in my spare time. There's a great little stream and trail right by my house with a population of deer and a few foxes. The deer are very protected in there and thus have lost a lot of their fear of humans, so I've been able to get quite close.

In mid-July I had an amazing vacation week: A Norwegian cruise with my family! The trip actually started with a day in London where we got to go see some sights before heading to our cruise. I was thrilled to see Battersea Bridge, a notable location for fans of Le Carre's George Smiley books. Our cruise had one stop in Belgium and three stops in Norway, and it was soooo beautiful! On my birthday we had a bike tour through Olden, during which my brothers and I (+ the tour guide) jumped into a freezing cold lake! Definitely a highlight of the trip for me, in addition to the copious amounts of food I consumed. Somehow I performed evasive maneuvers on jet lag coming home so going right back to work on Monday after getting in at midnight wasn't horrible (huge W).

Just this week I discovered a huge area of community garden plots at one of the nearby parks, so cute! The tomatoes looked especially yummy, I was inspired to start my own garden if I could. Will definitely be going back there to explore a bit more. I was particularly amused by a few older ladies who were going around picking whatever herbs and stuff were sticking out past the fences (fair game, I guess!).

Oh, how I do miss Florida, though. I have often found myself daydreaming about spanish moss, oak and pine trees, golden hour, the Hawthorne trail, my bike, and my favorite hometown bakery and deli. But my time in Virginia has been great so far, and i'm continuing to look forward to all that is in store for me!

Some fast facts to wrap it up: Currently Reading: Every Living Thing (last book of the James Herriot series), and The Midwife (Jennifer Worth) Currently Eating: Trader Joe's Beef & Queso bowl (so good omg) Currently Listening: Project Hail Mary soundtrack Currently Watching: The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel (but I’m almost finished nooo).

Miss you all! - Sara M
