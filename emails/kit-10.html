Hello to all you lovely people!! Happy July, aka the month of afternoon thunderstorms, and my birthday month (woohoo!). I hope your summers have been filled with lots of air conditioning and frozen treats. This is gonna be a long one, because I love to yap, and I’m currently sipping my coffee, so get comfy as I type my life away in run-on sentence format.

Starting with the most important post-grad journey, I went on a 2.5 week trip to Europe with my family!! (mom, dad, and older sister).

We started off in Iceland for a 24 hour layover, and used that time to explore. We got to see the most BEAUTIFUL views, and cute puffins!! It felt like I was standing on the edge of the world, quite insane. Highly recommend Iceland.

Next we stopped in Zürich, Switzerland. Beautiful city, and so many art/design museums!! Being at the birthplace of Swiss design made my little heart so happy.

From there, we took a train through the alps from Chur, Switzerland to Northern Italy. GORGEOUS. VIEWS. I have never seen mountains this big. It was AMAZING.

Italy took up the bulk of our trip. We stopped in Venice for two days (architecture was beautiful, so much history!!), then went off to the Chianti and Montepulciano regions of Tuscany. My family is reeeeally into wine, so we toured about 6 different vineyards and even stayed at an old barn in a Chianti vineyard! The countryside was absolutely stunning, and the food and wine was SO DELICIOUS. My foodie family and I ate endless ragu, tagliatelle, cheese, meat, tiramisu, panna cotta, and so much red wine. Gluttony consumed me entirely in Tuscany. I felt like a blob every day.

After eating too much pasta and drinking way too much red wine, we finally made our way to Lisbon, Portugal. And honestly, this might have been my favorite city of our whole trip. The Tuscan countryside was wonderful, but I missed the options that come from staying in a big city (mainly the option of iced coffee, I’m a creature of habit). The buildings in Lisbon were all decorated with colorful tiles, and we learned so much about the deep history of Portugal on a Sailboat ride through the Tagus river. It also helps that I LOVE seafood, and Lisbon is known for it!


After that long adventure, I landed back home in Tallahassee for a week, then immediately got in the car with my parents and drove 10 hours to Durham, NC to help my sister move into her new home as she starts her post-medical school residency at Duke (smarty pants I know). Durham is beautiful, but definitely just as hot as Florida is right now. I mostly spent my week there doing free labor by painting tables and scrubbing bathroom tiles, because I’m a wonderful sister, of course.

And now… Finally, my adventures come to a close as I rest at home, indefinitely, until my seemingly endless job search is over. I’m so grateful to have lovely parents that will provide for me during my unemployment, but in all honesty, I gotta move somewhere else soon. The great capital city of Tallahassee doesn’t quite capture the life I was hoping for post-grad. I’ve been practically living on Linkedin every day, and the people were not lying when they said the job market is brutal right now… Despite this, I persist! Nothing will dull my sparkle, not even unemployment 💪 Hopefully the next time you guys hear from me, I’ll have a stable job. This whole stay-at-home-daughter thing is DEFINITELY not a long term goal…

In other news, My birthday is coming up in less than a week! (where my leo baddies 🦁) And as I turn 22 I can’t help but get excited about what the future could be. I’m an open book right now, and though searching for a job is rather excruciating, there is some excitement and joy that I find in an uncertain future. Anything could happen!!! ANYTHING!! And I think that’s kinda beautiful!

Here’s your reminder that you shouldn’t have to have everything figured out. You’re still young, learning to navigate through post-college life, and you have a looooong future ahead of you (my daily affirmations). And you have a bachelor’s degree. Booyah!

I miss you all dearly! Stay fun, take care, and cheers to the future!! 🥂

Emily M 🐕
