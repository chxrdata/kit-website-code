What’s good!! I’m coming to you live from Boston, Massachusetts. Yes, work (‘47 Brand)

What’s good!! I’m coming to you live from Boston, Massachusetts. Yes, work (‘47 Brand) is good. I’m getting lots of cool apparel and headwear projects for events such as the NBA Mexico City Game, NBA All Star Game, Rose Bowl, Peach Bowl… and more. I’m really happy I talked to my supervisor, Sab, about doing more so I would actually get production projects rather than “intern exercises.” Plus… I got to make the headwear intern jealous a little bit by designing headwear in addition to apparel LOL. At HeadCount, I got to see Ariana Grande for FREE and it was incredible. Honestly I’m a little tired of that job but going to shows is worth it. I just found out I’m seeing Harry Styles and that’s everything to me. I almost cried at my desk. But work is all-consuming right now so I’ve really been trying to embrace the joyful moments outside of it (reconciling with the fact that I’m going to have to work 8.5 hours a day for a looooong time). Here are some things I have done OUTSIDE of work!

Last week, I went to a woman and queer-owned brewery for a draft rootbeer and the Women’s Professional Baseball League game. Totally out of my comfort zone but the people watching the game were exactly the people I have been looking for. Plus I have a team to cheer for– the Boston Hunters! I’m completely obsessed with the league right now.

I drove to Rhode Island twice! Once to Providence to go to the RISD art museum and once to Newport to go to the beach. I love being driving-distance from so many states. I want to go to New Hampshire, Connecticut, and Maine but I might wait until I am hired full-time– knock on wood. And I have a train ticket to NYC to see my friends in a couple of weeks! à la Marlie, I look forward to seeing what gay bars I find myself in.

I have been getting so much coffee. Viza Cafe in Jamaica Plain is my favorite and I am becoming a regular there. Trying to get the attention of the barista… I think she’s starting to recognize me. There’s also a cafe on my street called Green T, named after the public transit, and I like going there to interact with the locals!

I took the train into the city a couple of times, but my favorite time was going to the Boston Museum of Fine Arts and then stumbling into Fenway Porchfest, where a bunch of locals and students from Northeastern and Emerson etc. were playing live music.

I’ve also gone to a Red Sox game at which the Scots hit on my supervisor, gotten my thirds pierced, made zines about experiences I’ve had here, planned a zine with Del, gone to the city beach to read, explored local bookstores, walked about the Harvard University Arnold Arboretum, had a James Spader movie marathon (Secretary changed my life), had a parallel Toy Story 5 movie (shoutout Josh), saw Spiderman… which was so so good… and tuned into my highschoolself to make a pov playlist: “pov: ur dating lupe garcia” from A League of Their Own. Related, the aforementioned headwear intern called me out cause I asked “wait– have you seen a League of Their Own?” and she said, “Macy. You’ve brought this up multiple times.” SORRY and I’m not going to shut up about the show and we’re literally sports design interns so lock in, get gay, and support Women’s baseball.

And because I want to use up all the words possible I have some notes from the office(s):

  • Andrew is listening to Noah kahan in his air pods louder than anyone has ever listened to Noah kahan is he hurting
  • Someone just claimed the offspring was the ~greatest~ band of ~all time~ Rocco is not having it
  • So Rocco does play drums I’ve just learned. We talked about bass before but no no, he is multifaceted
  • I forgot I have to do other sports besides baseball, football, and basketball lol. Tell me why I have to make a graphic for GOLF.
  • Oh my lord in a flat bed ford if I have to hear Jamie say “I didn’t understand” and “you might have to re-work the graphic” ONE MORE TIME. I AM GOING TO QUIT!!!! EVERYONE SAY “JAMIE GET IT TOGETHER!!!”
  • I’m buzzed out of my mind off Dunkin iced coffee making Red Sox merch (concept) I think I’m a Bostoner now

I haven’t been this tired since my senior year of high school but when things are good, they are really, really good. Man, and every week I look forward to keepintouch so I hope y’all sign up. I love my friends!!
