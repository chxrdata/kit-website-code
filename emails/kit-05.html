France Face to Face by Mark Barlow

Greetings from France!

This summer I’ve had the awesome opportunity to go on another cultural exchange internship with a church. A group of us from GNV have the job here to help out with a small church and spark conversations about spirituality with locals. If anyone is interested in our beliefs, we also help them take their next steps to follow Jesus whether that’s reading the Bible, joining the church, or just learning more.

This work is super important to me because, as many of you know, the love of Jesus changed my life in a radical way. God showed His love for us by sending Jesus to die for our brokenness, so we can be made whole. Knowing this is a free gift that saved me from hopelessness gave me a friendship with a creator who loves me encourages me to be expressive about my faith so others can be introduced to the gift as well. As always, if anyone is interested in talking more about faith, I would love to have a conversation with you when I get back in July. :)

We’ve done a ton of cool things while here, from taking French classes to seeing all the sights to working on a church construction project. My favorite by far has to be our visit to Monet’s Home & Gardens.



I still have many many museums to visit and explore, but on the day after my birthday we absolutely had to visit the home of my favorite artist. When I stepped into the house, I was blown away. There were so many real Monet pieces one after another lined up in the first room and around the rest of the house. The line of people was moving quickly, or else I would’ve spent hours in there. Around the house you’ll also find a few Renoir pieces and a large collection of Asian art. The kitchen and other rooms are also colorful and beautifully designed.

Outside of the house there is a large beautiful flower garden full of roses, lilies, and many other blossoms. There are also chickens which I think is really cute. You know the iconic green bridge? I walked all around that area. There was a sunshower over a family of swans and it was magical! What’s even crazier is that this was one of the milder gardens in France. We’ll likely see even more elaborate ones as the trip goes on.

My SIM card here has a different number, but when I get back, I’d love to show pictures of everything to anyone who’d want to see. And when I get inside the Louvre and Museé d’Orsay, I’m sure I’ll have a million more.

Another fun thing is that I got a job offer while here—NCCER in Alachua for their layout designer position! I haven’t accepted yet, but as I am hoping to stay in GNV, I likely will.

I’ll wrap this up because I’m already submitting late (sorry Del! You’re awesome :). But to the question about what brings me joy, the answer is Jesus; seeing God answer prayers and bring joy to the people around me is the best thing I’ve known.
