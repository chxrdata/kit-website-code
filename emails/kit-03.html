Sanibel, FL A Seagull & Clouds

Hello guys,

Hope everybody is enjoying the summer and going on all kinds of amazing adventures!

I’ve started the next adventure in my life with an internship working for “Ding” Darling Wildlife Society out in Sanibel, Florida. The organization is a nonprofit branch of the local J.N. “Ding” Darling National Wildlife Refuge. We do outreach/events and contact donors using the funds for water/wildlife conservation, as well as education and advocacy.

I started in mid-May, so I haven't even done a full month here, but I've completed the onboarding process and have started posting on the organization’s social media and preparing to do the donor newsletter. I've also been making design proposals for merch that can be put in the refuge’s nature store. Next week, I’ll be working on the refuge property so that I get to meet the staff and rangers, who maintain the actual park and talk to visitors.

While most of my experience so far has been learning the ropes, there are some exciting mornings when I go out with some of my coworkers to take photos and videos of the wildlife! I've been able to see so many cool animals and even some new birds! I’m trying to sneak some of my personal photos into the social media posts I've been making. There is so much wildlife to see, my desk faces a canal, and some days you can see manatees swimming past the mangroves. I’m so delighted to be working somewhere with such biodiversity.

Outside of work, I do a lot of biking and wandering in the natural spaces. I'm trying to get used to this new environment its wildly different from student life. Everything closes early in the day, and there are no streetlights, so after sunset it is beautifully dark. There are tons of bike paths and wildlife trails, though. So most of my time after work is spent using what's left of the daylight to bike or walk the coast looking for shells and cool birds. A week after I arrived, a colony of black skimmers moved in on one of the beaches, so I've been going to check on their nests every once in a while. I'm hoping to get some nice pictures of the chicks once they hatch.

I miss everybody and hope everyone is off doing awesome stuff or taking a well-deserved break. While you're back home or on your travels, always feel free to send me pictures of the cool wildlife you see. And if you find yourself in Sanibel over the summer, let me know, and I'll give ya a tour of the refuge!
