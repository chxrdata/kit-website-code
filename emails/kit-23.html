Hi friends!!! I hope you all are enjoying this crisp cool fall weather and prepping for Thanksgiving, or perhaps you’re already prepping for Christmas. I won’t judge. It’s been a while since you’ve heard from me, so I figured I would give y’all a big ole update. A lot has changed since my last newsletter in July, so strap in for this one.

Let’s start from the beginning, as the most obvious change lies in the headline of this newsletter. Yes, the rumors are true, I’m currently living in another Gville, aka Greenville South Carolina! After an agonizing job search and over 75 jobs applied to in the span of 3 months, I finally landed a full-time Junior Graphic Designer role at a local Greenville publishing agency called Community Journals. HOORAH!! I’m so lucky to have been offered this stable job and have the opportunity to live in a brand new city.

For those that may be curious about my role, I basically design and process all print and digital ads for our agency’s publications, including one newspaper and four magazines. The newspaper comes out weekly, and two of those magazines come out monthly, so you can imagine the deadlines can get preeetty chaotic and unpredictable between last-minute clients and endless edits. But hey, I’ll always thrive in a little bit of chaos. I’ve been learning SO much about print production, Dropbox file management, and spending my days in an Adobe InDesign and Acrobat loop. My work environment is really great, and it feels amazing to have my hard work recognized (and compensated). We have a sales team, editorial team, and a design team, all relatively small but that makes the office kind of feel like one big dysfunctional family. I’m also really grateful that I get to work in office Monday-Thursday, and remote on Fridays, which really saves me from completely losing my marbles. Yay for full time employment!!

Greenville itself is a really cute city. I have a studio apartment right on the edge of downtown, which is the main attraction of the city. Greenville is known for a trailing river that turns into a waterfall in the heart of downtown, and honestly I understand the hype, it’s pretty majestic. The food scene here is also popping which, as a certified foodie, I can definitely appreciate, quite similar to Gainesville. There are four Michelin guide restaurants and one Michelin star restaurant, all on my bucket list to try soon. And of course, this wouldn’t be an Emily M newsletter without a coffee mention! There’s a bunch of coffee shops and restaurants right near my office, so you can imagine I’ve been blowing a moderate amount of my paycheck trying seasonal lattes and getting chorizo breakfast burritos for lunch. (Did y’all know San Diego style breakfast burritos have HASHBROWNS INSIDE??? My life has been changed for the better).

Since moving here, I’ve really been throwing myself into a lot of social interactions in an attempt to make some friends. Full time employment is basically a game of figuring out your own work-life balance standards and how much stuff you can truly fit into a week without dreading work the next day. As an adult, I’ve realized that if you want to make friends outside of work, you have to capitalize on your hobbies. Maximize common-interest friendship output, if you will. So far, I’ve joined two dance groups (working on a third), and made a lot of my new friends through dance. I also made some connections in the queer scene here got to DJ a set at a queer club night recently, which was kind of a side quest dream come true!

As 2026 wraps up relatively soon (that thought just sent a shiver down my spine), I can’t help but wonder what January me would think of me now. Life changes so fast, it’s hard to keep up sometimes. I had genuinely no idea where I would end up post-grad, so take it from me when I say sometimes no plan is the best plan. Hell, I never thought I would end up living in South Carolina post-grad, but it’s been such a wonderful experience! Let life take you where you need to go, with some level of initiative and dedication, of course. I know this, because I’ve created quite the busy schedule for myself. Shoutout to Del for letting me push back this newsletter twice due to the chaotic nature of my life currently, I would be sick of me if I were them. Currently writing this on my lunch break while working from home, lol.

I think I’ve probably surpassed the word limit, so thank you for reading my blabberous update! I hope all of you are thriving and I can’t wait to hear more life updates soon. If you ever find yourself passing through Greenville, let’s link up! :) Cheers!
