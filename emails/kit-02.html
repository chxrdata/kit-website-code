Gainesville, FL Girl's Girl by MUNA

Hiii everyone!! How exciting this post-grad life has already been. Traveling and exploring, but also now knowing what millennials mean when they say “adulting is hard.”

Three days after walking the stage, I flew out of Tampa to Ireland– the motherland. I got my first tattoo in Dublin after sleeping for about three hours, saw lots of cows, sheep, and horses, listened to trad music in local bars, and ate so much meat and potatoes I thought I was going to turn into a potato.

My favorite part was the Book of Kells. If you don’t know, the Book of Kells is an illustrated medieval manuscript made by Celtic monks, so I got to see medieval inks and pigments, a highly decorative illuminated alphabet, and learn about medieval symbology. I felt like a little kid in a candy shop, giggling and pointing out all these fantastic elements. That’s pretty much how I felt all over Ireland. I wish I had more than a week to see the sights, but it’ll have to be another trip.

The past week, I’ve been back in Gainesville to work on my remote internship until my lease runs out. I started with a Tom Petty Weekend poster for Heartwood Soundstage, which will be my second time designing the poster and I’m super excited about it. Then May 19th was my first day working at HeadCount, which is a nonprofit that collaborates with musicians and music festivals to encourage voter registration. It’s definitely fulfilling an emotional need for me, as someone who loves music and activism. I’ve been told I might even get to see Harry Styles with them, which would be huge for me. But… in an unexpected turn of events… I was also offered a full-time internship with ‘47 in Westwood, Massachusetts. What?? Macy doing sports merchandise?? Maybe! The idea of getting up and moving to Massachusetts in mere weeks is quite thrilling, and hey, they actually pay pretty decently. However, it’s a tall order to figure out the logistics and it is really stressing me out. I hear it’s good to have choices! But when people say that, do they realize then you actually have to make a decision? I’m going to figure out how to do both HeadCount and ‘47, though, because that’s the dream. Plus, after being in Ireland, I have just been yearning to go somewhere else. Get out of Gainesville, out of Florida. So this could be my chance.

In other news, MUNA IS HAPPENING. They released their new album on my last day in Ireland and I have been streaming it daily ever since. This is huge– you should have seen the pure joy of Jess and I listening to “Wannabeher” in the production room when it dropped. My pre-ordered record arrived in the mail at my house, too… I get to retrieve it this weekend and I’m chomping at the bit to see the marbled red vinyl. Next up, I’m pulling the trigger and buying a ticket to see them in Atlanta in September. Can you believe they’re playing the day after my birthday?? They did that for me, I swear. If anyone wants to go with me, please let me know. I’m serious. I’m otherwise going to be dragging my parents to see my lesbian synthpop band and although I think that would be really funny, maybe one of you are secret MUNA-lovers and want to go. I urge you to REVEAL YOURSELF! Or if you’re into non-men, look up a picture of Naomi, Josette, or Katie and you will be persuaded. And go ahead and give the song at the top of the page a listen.

It wouldn’t be a Macy update without physical media, so get this: I just bought SIX DVDs at 2nd and Charles the other day. The Karate Kid, High Fidelity, The Sisterhood of the Traveling Pants, Whip It, After Hours, and Fast Times at Ridgemont High. Aka, a near perfect Macy DVD haul. As far as records go, I was a pre-grad shopaholic and got MUNA’s debut, Hozier’s Wasteland, Baby!, The Veronicas, Olivia Rodrigo Live at Glastonbury (courtesy of Josh), of course Kiss All the Time, Disco Occasionally, and even more. I even bought my first CD! The Chicks! No cassettes in a minute, though. Soon, I’m sure.

So what’s next? Maybe I’ll be in Massachusetts, maybe I’ll become a huge sports fan, maybe I’ll be seeing Harry Styles in New York or going to pride, or maybe I’ll do a complete switch up, go home to Tampa and start up my old baking business… I’m in a huge closet full of hats and I’ll be trying them all on. Or, at least, trying to. Hopefully one has ‘47 embroidered on the side! Rock on.
