A HUGE HELLO FROM NEW YORK CITY!!

(Hoping you read that in my loud, booming voice, I KNOW you all miss so dearly lol. I am most certainly missing all of you loads!)

I started my internship with Universal Music Group a month ago, it’s essentially the same exact job I was doing at Sony Music last summer, just different company workflows and artist rosters. I’ve been doing a lot of packaging design as of recently, both freelance and at work, for vinyls. On top of that, some cover art and random digital assets. The vibes in the office are REALLY great though; it truly reminds me a lot of the studio. I'll paint the picture.

All the interns, which there are around 15 of us on my floor, do not have desks. We all sit around this lush teal/emerald-colored velvet sofa. It's a modular one that surrounds a small coffee table covered in music-related books; it's dubbed the “Creative Pit.” The overall energy is super collaborative and high-energy, much like the studio. And of course, you know the drill: when it's time to lock in, it gets quite silent, but nonetheless still a really fun time every day at work.

We, the interns, were all given a field trip day to the recording studio many of the artists within the label and company record at, and we got to listen to a bunch of songs in the booth on Dolby Atmos. If you are unsure of what that means, Dolby Atmos is an advanced 3D surround-sound tech system that makes listeners feel as if they are sitting inside the audio. It's crazy!

In other news, I tried matcha for the first time while hanging out with some friends in the city a few days ago, and I can still confirm it truly still tastes like grass to me. Sorry, matcha lovers. I have also been eating copious amounts of really yummy Thai food here. It's hard to find food places here that have bad food; honestly, everything's been so good!

During city adventures, I was also lucky enough to score some tickets to be a live audience member for the filming of both Late Night with Seth Meyers and The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon, which was super cool. I had my 5 seconds of fame (more like half a second) in the two times the camera did a wide shot of the audience lol. My favorite guest of all the ones we were able to see from both nights was Paul Rudd. He's been a favorite actor of mine for a while, and he was absolutely hilarious that night on Fallon.

As far as what is next from here, it's very much unknown, but I am more than ready for an adventure and to see how things will change as I make my way into post-grad life. I could not be more excited to see where the wind takes me. One thing that has not changed and is for certain is my raging Diet Coke addiction, which unfortunately has NOT seen itself out in my post-grad journey, and I have a feeling it will not be any different anytime soon.

Until next time, and don't be a stranger!
