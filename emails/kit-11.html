Miss you all and it has been so cool seeing you all doing awesome amazing things post-grad (yes I’m a proud linkedin stalker)

Life since graduation for me has been defined by the unemployed friend stereotype. Since April I haven’t spent more than 5 days in the same place which has been so awesome.

I spent May backpacking from Paris to Greece, sleeping anywhere from a hostel to a bus to an airport floor, to not sleeping at all… We managed to visit 10 countries, seeing the cities, exploring the countryside, and trying a LOT of gelato. Highlights from Europe included some hikes in the Austrian alps, having dinner with strangers we met while hiking in Montenegro, and crossing multiple borders via AlbanianBus.

June I returned to the US, crashing on various friends and family members' couches as I road tripped around the west from California through to Montana. Then I ended up heading back to my parents in Utah where after a few days of looking at Linkedin I decided I was not done being unemployed and spontaneously decided there is no better time than now to buy a one way ticket to Costa Rica.

I found a hostel that I could volunteer with and found myself living in the Caribbean town of Puerto Viejo and learning how to order coffee and grocery shop in Spanish. Then heartbreakingly the host ended up asking me to pay more rent and do different work than agreed upon, and due to my jobless status that was something I simply could not afford, so after two weeks, it was time for me to go.

Currently I’m back out west, exploring some of the nearby parks and getting to know Utah very well. In terms of career professional job stuff, I’ve been freelancing and really enjoying the flexibility it gives me to run around. I have a few interviews I’ve done recently so I hope to be legitimately employed soon, but until then who knows where I’ll be next…

ANYWAYS I hope you all are doing so great and look forward to our paths crossing as we all continue to take on post-grad life!!
